*Title card: December 2024*

* MARLOW: You punched through DIMENSIONS just to get a god damned Thanksgiving TURKEY?!

*Open in the Polaris Building, in the study at the top we've seen before: Marlow's office. Rich wood, art, bookcases full of old tomes, the works. She's slamming both her hands on the table, and despite the fact that her entire body is obscured in robes and cloth wrappings and a mask, she is very clearly seething.*

*Magus, for his part, is refusing to give in to the framing of "being called to the principal's office". In full techwear regalia, he's pulled his headset's visor down over his eyes for the first time, partially obscuring his expression. Loa the racing synth (and de-facto arena engineer) is beside him; Exime the combat synth (and de-facto arena security) is standing on the sidelines between the two, her arms folded.*

* MARLOW: May I remind you that your so-called arena runs in this city under *my* permission. Which, may I *further* remind you, was given [under the condition that you wouldn't start recklessly exposing Blue Arcadia to other worlds](https://www.dustbreaker.co/2023/06/26/system-intro-2.html). ...What the HELL were you doing?!
* MAGUS: [cautiously choosing his words] I performed an experiment.
* MARLOW: [derisively] An *experiment*. An experiment is a controlled test to prove something. What exactly did you *prove* here? Whether Rathalos meat tastes good with sriracha?!

*Exime glances at Marlow, not seeming to entirely understand. Magus steps forward and tosses a holographic datapad onto her desk. It's displaying two images of a woman: one pixelated, like a 3D model rendered through a low-res screen, where she's sitting on a bench of red wood, and the other a very similar shot, but photorealistic, her pose more natural, less stiff.*

*Marlow picks up the holopad and examines it. Even from behind her mask, her loose grip communicates disdain. Exime is craning her head around, and Loa is looking up at Magus with his monoeye, concerned.*

* MARLOW: Explain.
* MAGUS: *That* is Ayame of Kamura. Approximately six months prior to the start of the Rampage, while hunting a Rathalos, she is *supposed* to suffer a major injury to her left leg. She never fully heals, and her career as a hunter ends there.
* LOA: [putting the pieces together] So the Rathalos you had the heroes fight...
* MAGUS: A very specific Rathalos, yes.
* EXIME: ...What do you mean "she's *supposed* to"?
* [MAGUS opens his mouth, looking meaningfully toward MARLOW as if checking with her, but he doesn't get a word out--]
* MARLOW: [loudly, pointedly, cutting him off] I see.
* MAGUS: [annoyed, but not contesting her implicit command here] I'm not convinced you do. It's been three weeks now, and the only change here is that she's still up and about.
* MARLOW: And how long do you intend to monitor that for unintended consequences? A month? A year? A decade? What are you going to do when something goes wrong, because she wasn't in the right place at the right time? [it's clearly hitting a sore spot] Who the hell are *you* to decide for an entire world what's better than normal?
* MAGUS: [finally snapping] Who are *you* to decide that whatever happens first is the "right" path? If we *can* help, don't we have an obligation to?
* [EXIME and LOA trade a confused look. This seems to be over their heads. We can't see MAGUS's eyes, but his visor is glaring.]
* MARLOW: I have an *obligation* to the synths here, and no one else. Especially not you.

*Marlow straightens up, picks up the holopad by the corner, and tosses it onto the floor, clattering in Magus's direction.*

* MARLOW: Get the hell out of my city. And take your hole-punch with you.

*The two stare at each other for a long moment, visor to mask. Magus doesn't exactly back down, but does turn.*

* MAGUS: All right, then. It's been a pleasure. [walks out]
* LOA: [chasing after him and down the hall] Magus? Magus, you can't be serious, what about the arena? Magus?

*Cut to black.*

*Title card: July 2026.*

*Open on the arena floor. Loa is in the audience, leaning over the railing. The stands are mostly empty, lightly peppered with synths; they're watching a fight between two other synths. One is Exime, de-facto arena security; the other is a large orange-and-yellow cargo-loader synth. The two seem about evenly matched-- when the loader lands a hit, it sends Exime flying, but she manages to tech the landing and launch right back in.*

*And off in that section of the stands, behind Loa, several heroes are sitting together.*

* DRAGOSHI: I'm not gonna lie, chief, I'm gettin' real tired of 1v1 fistfights every week.
* HERRINGWAY: It's certainly not as dramatic...
* LOA: I know, but without Magus's RCP, I can't get the Hero Drivers to authorize... [chuckles weakly, a mechanical blooping noise that only vaguely resembles it] I think the only one having fun like this is Exime.

*She is, in fact, having a lot of fun dodging and weaving. It seems more and more like she's toying with this loader-synth.*

* LOA: Oh well. Same time next week...?

*There's a general mumble of agreement, more out of boredom than anything. The heroes filter down the stairs to the green room, changing back into their civilian clothes. In the back of the shot, Exime roars and yells, holding up a severed loader-arm.*

*Back in the green room, Loa is joining the heroes in their ritual. Similarly to one hero washing their face, Loa is imitating it, washing his featureless visor, clearly not understanding the point of doing it.*

*Midway through, Loa stops, suddenly frozen.*

* RUE: Uh... you okay there?

*Just as Rue reaches out to touch them, Loa's speakers blast a burst of static. Loa's body jolts upright.*

* VOICE: ...You guys still there? I got a job for you. ...You didn't think you'd be fighting holograms forever, did ya? I'm gonna show you why I made this whole stupid thing to begin with.

*The heroes look at each other, confused-- but with a loud twisting distorted-synth noise-- a ragged hole blasts through the fabric of space-time. On the other side: a sunny city street, and Magus peeks through, leaning across with a shit-eating grin.*

* MAGUS: C'mon, you beautiful bastards. Let's go save a life.

Um, hey everybody! Loa here, cause Magus is a little busy. This month, we're on our first excursion outside the borders of Blue Arcadia, off into another world! Magus has a plan to fix all this other world's problems, I guess...? We don't know exactly what we're gonna run into, but he says this:

"The sun is beautiful, the sky is clear and clean, and I hope you know how to skate, because today, our goal is simple: get to the bottom of this hill intact!

There's a lot of things that are gonna try to stop us: big chunky guys, small floaty guys, all of 'em robots of one stripe or another. Lotsa stuff, but it should be simple for heroes with your level of experience. I'm not super worried.

It might be a little bumpy here and there, but just sit back and enjoy the chaos!"

That's all he left me, so your guess is as good as mine. Either way, our operation begins on the 26th, at 7 PM your-time!
